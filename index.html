The Art of Cement Tiles

A heritage deeply woven into the architectural fabric of Beirut. Our tiles tell a story of resilience, craftsmanship, and timeless Mediterranean design.

Hand-Pressed & Sun-Cured

Traditional Lebanese cement tiles—historically known as *blat tla22*—are individually hand-poured into metal molds, compressed using hydraulic presses, and left to cure under the warm Mediterranean sun. This slow, artisanal process makes each piece completely unique, durable, and rich in depth of color.

We preserve this timeless method while partnering with modern Lebanese designers to re-envision classic geometric motifs, creating styles that feel right at home in modern Beirut apartments or rustic mountain villas.
